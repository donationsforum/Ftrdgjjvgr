    How to play
    1. On your turn, claim any empty dot.
    2. Your dots connect to your own neighbours, sideways, up and down, or diagonally.
    3. The first player to link enough dots in one straight line wins. Block your rivals as you go.
    4. Touch: on a big board, tap a dot once. On a crowded board, tap to aim, then tap again to place.
    5. Keyboard: arrow keys move, Enter or Space claims.
    6. Sound: tap the speaker icon to mute or unmute effects.
